Cookies Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Short version

This website does not use cookies. We don't run analytics, advertising pixels, or any tracking script — nothing on this site reads or writes a cookie, and nothing here builds a profile of you across visits.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store, usually to remember who you are between page loads (for things like login sessions, shopping carts, or analytics). Because we don't use any of that on this site, there's nothing to consent to and no cookie banner to show you.

What we do use

This site loads two web fonts from external providers — Google Fonts and Fontshare — so the page renders in the right typeface. Loading a font this way isn't a cookie: it's a one-off request your browser makes directly to that provider's servers, which (like any web request) may log your IP address on their end as standard server behaviour. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising cookies in future, we'll update this page and add a consent banner that lets you accept or decline before anything non-essential loads. Until then, there's genuinely nothing to manage.

Controlling cookies generally

Even though this site doesn't set any, most browsers let you view, block, or delete cookies set by other sites via their settings — look for "Privacy" or "Cookies" in your browser's preferences if you'd like to manage this globally.
